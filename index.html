Ready

Stay informed and prepare your household.

Preparedness checklist
  • Sign up for LPC Alerts, enable government alerts, and use Watch Duty.
  • Plan for people, pets, photos, papers and pills.
  • Prepare your property and a go kit with protective clothing and masks.
  • Know multiple routes out. Don’t hesitate when ordered to evacuate.

Set · Pre-evacuation

Voluntary evacuation. Evacuation orders are likely. Be ready to leave at a moment’s notice.

Go · Evacuate now

Leave immediately. Imminent threat to life. The area is closed to public access.

Shelter · Shelter in place

Remain inside or move indoors when instructed. Follow the incident’s specific protective actions.

Access Impact Area

Road closures may limit or cut off access. Check incident details. This is not an evacuation or shelter order.

Planning

Planning areas are informational, not an evacuation or shelter order. Check incident details.
